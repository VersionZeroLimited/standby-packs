# Emergency — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 November 2026

Emergency is an offline survival guide app made by Version Zero Limited ("we", "us"). This page explains what the app does with your information. It is written to be read — the whole point of this app is that your information stays yours.

## 1. The short version

**Emergency collects no personal data — from anyone, ever.** The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party trackers. Its App Store privacy label reads "Data Not Collected", and that is exactly what it means.

## 2. What the app stores on your device

Emergency keeps a small amount of state in your device's local storage, including:

- which items you have ticked on your 72-hour bag checklist
- when you last reviewed your bag, which is used to schedule your six-month reminder
- which content packs you have downloaded

This information never leaves your device. It is not synced to a cloud service, not uploaded anywhere, and not visible to us. If you delete the app, it is deleted along with it.

## 3. How the Ask (AI) feature works

The Ask tab is powered by Apple Intelligence — the foundation models Apple builds into iOS. The model runs entirely on your device: your question, the guide content it reads, and the answer it gives you are all processed on the phone. No question ever leaves the device, there is no server-side AI, and we never see what you ask or what the app answers. (Ask requires a device that supports Apple Intelligence; the rest of the app works the same everywhere.)

## 4. Network use

Emergency is fully offline. The guides, search, the bag checklist and Ask all work with no connection at all.

The only network request the app ever makes is a content-pack download that you start yourself. Packs come from our public repository at https://github.com/VersionZeroLimited/standby-packs and are served by GitHub's CDN. When you download a pack, your request goes to GitHub, so GitHub's own privacy policy (https://privacy.github.com) applies to that request — just as it would if you opened the repository in your web browser. We receive nothing from it: no logs, no analytics, no identifiers. (Downloading the app itself from the App Store is handled by Apple under Apple's own policies.)

## 5. Local notifications

Emergency can schedule one optional reminder to review your grab bag every six months. The reminder is created and delivered entirely on your device. There are no push notifications, no notification servers, and nothing about you is sent anywhere to make it work. You can decline the reminder or turn it off at any time.

## 6. Children

Emergency collects no data from anyone — adults or children. There is nothing to opt out of, because nothing is collected and nothing is transmitted. A child using this app has exactly as much data collected as an adult: none.
